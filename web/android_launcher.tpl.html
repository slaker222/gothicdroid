About

GothicDroid

Android

The project

GothicDroid brings Gothic and Gothic II (and the Chronicles of Myrtana: Archolos mod) to Android phones: a launcher that finds or downloads the games, touch controls with an editable layout, graphics settings and a renderer made for mobile GPUs. It needs the game files of your own copy of the game.

Based on

GothicDroid is a fork of the open source engine OpenGothic by Try (MIT license).
