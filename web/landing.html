GothicDroid

Play Gothic 1 and Gothic 2 right in your browser, on Android and PC. This is a build of OpenGothic for the browser.

Gothic 1

Action RPG from 2001. You are a prisoner thrown into the penal colony of the Valley of Mines, which is sealed off by a magical Barrier. Join one of the three Camps (the Old Camp, the New Camp or the Brotherhood of the Sect) and look for a way to break the Barrier.

Gothic 2

The 2002 sequel. The hero wakes up after the Barrier has fallen, on the island of Khorinis, where orcs and dragons threaten the land. Explore the city, the farms and the Valley of Mines, join the Militia, the Mercenaries or the Mages, and prepare for the final battle.

The Chronicles of Myrtana: Archolos

You play Marvin, a young man who flees a coming war and arrives with his brother on the island of Archolos, which is said to be a safe place. It turns out to be far from safe. Rich merchants, the enforcers of King Rhobar II and crowds of refugees share the island. The game offers a new open world, over 170 quests with several ways to finish them and more than 60 hours of play.
