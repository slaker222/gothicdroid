GothicDroid

Android

What is this

GothicDroid is an Android fork of OpenGothic, an open source engine that runs the original Gothic and Gothic II (and mods such as Chronicles of Myrtana: Archolos) with a new renderer. It needs the game files of your own copy of the game.
